1. Who We Are and the Scope of This Policy

Sein is provided by Persein Inc. Our contact email for privacy and general service inquiries is hi@seinpai.com.

This version applies to services operated by Persein Inc., covering the Sein iOS App, Sein wearable devices used with it, the official website, and cloud and AI services that we operate and expressly identify as subject to this Policy. We plan to provide services in countries and regions where we have the necessary service capabilities and meet applicable local requirements. Actual availability is as announced on the App Store and by the product; this does not mean that services are already available in all countries and regions. Other platform versions, enterprise services, or independent third-party services will provide separate applicable explanations if their processing differs.

In this Policy, “you” means an individual using these services; “we,” “us,” and “our” mean Persein Inc. “Processing” includes collection, storage, and analysis locally on a device, as well as transmission to servers and cloud processing. Local processing is still processing of personal information.

Accepting the User Agreement or reading this Policy does not authorize every optional form of data processing. For features requiring separate consent, explicit permission, or system permissions, we will explain these separately and obtain the appropriate authorization.

2. What You Need to Know First

  1. Sein can process audio, audio transcripts, photos, chat content, and location, health, calendar, and motion information that you authorize. The specific scope depends on the features you use.
  2. Raw audio is generally transcribed and analyzed locally on your phone, but may be temporarily stored. Recordings you actively save and diagnostics you separately enable may have different retention rules.
  3. Generating AI replies and summaries requires sending relevant content to the cloud for processing. Before the first transmission, we will clearly explain the recipients, data scope, and purposes and obtain the appropriate permission.
  4. Voiceprints, health information, precise location, and sensitive information within content require special protection. Refusing nonessential authorization affects only the corresponding features.
  5. We do not sell your personal information, use your private records for targeted advertising, or use health, voiceprint, or movement information for advertising profiles. Except for data voluntarily provided under an invitation-only program with separate explicit consent as described in Section 5.5, we do not use your private content for model training, fine-tuning, or human quality evaluation, and require processors to comply with corresponding purpose restrictions.
  6. Protection by user keys, unreadability through the operational administration system, and processing according to authorization are prerequisites for enabling cloud storage and processing of private content. We will not enable the corresponding cloud features before these protection requirements are met; see Section 5.
  7. You can manage authorizations and request access to, correction, export, or deletion of personal information. Turning off synchronization, signing out, uninstalling the App, unbinding a device, and deleting an account have different effects; see Section 10.

3. What Information We Process and Why

We process information according to the features actually provided. Listing a category in this Policy does not mean that we collect that category by default. Before adding a processing purpose or materially expanding the scope, we will update our disclosures and obtain authorization as required.

3.1 Accounts, Devices, and Identity Association

Depending on the sign-in method you use, we process the phone number you provide and verification-code verification results, email address, nickname, profile image, or account identifier returned by the sign-in platform. Sign-in methods you do not use will not be enabled as a result.

To distinguish devices, associate records, manage quotas, and protect accounts, we also process application-generated anonymous or pseudonymous identifiers, account identifiers, device binding identifiers, device model, system and App versions, firmware version, and necessary connection status. Anonymous use may still generate linkable identifiers and does not necessarily mean the data is no longer identifiable.

We do not require you to provide Sein with your Apple ID password, bank card password, or passwords for other services.

3.2 Audio, Transcription, and Voice Analysis

When you enable device listening, phone voice input, active conversation, or recording, Sein processes sounds collected by the microphone. Audio may be transmitted between the phone and wearable device over Bluetooth.

To provide transcription, distinguish speakers, and generate records, we process raw audio segments, transcription results, collection times, duration, recognition confidence, and necessary acoustic features. Raw audio may wait for synchronization on the wearable device or be temporarily stored on the phone to complete synchronization, transcription, and analysis. Completion of transcription does not mean the audio is necessarily deleted immediately.

Raw recordings from passive listening are not used as routine inputs for cloud inference. If a released feature requires uploading active-conversation segments, segments you choose to save, or diagnostic samples, we will first explain the specific segments, recipients, purposes, and retention periods within that feature and obtain separate authorization. Agreeing to this Policy alone does not authorize uploading all recordings.

3.2.1 Continuous Collection, Background Operation, and Pausing

The wearable device's collection status is separate from whether the phone App is in the foreground. After continuous listening, automatic photography, or background location is enabled, the corresponding collection may continue while the phone is locked or the App is not in the foreground. Raw audio, audio transcripts, and photos have different storage and upload rules and cannot be treated as a single data category.

Before enabling collection, we will explain collection methods, triggering conditions, operational indicators, and how to pause and turn it off. You can stop the corresponding collection using officially provided device controls or App switches. Merely disabling the phone's microphone permission does not necessarily stop the wearable device's own recording.

On the App's device page, you can select a perception mode: “Present” enables listening and automatic photography; “Listen” keeps listening enabled and turns off automatic photography; “Standby” sends the connected device an instruction to pause recording and automatic photography. Standby is temporary: collection may resume after disconnection and reconnection or a device restart, so please reconfirm the device status. Pausing synchronization of historical data does not pause device collection.

3.3 Voiceprints and Speaker Information

When you enable voiceprint enrollment and related speaker recognition features, we process enrollment audio and voiceprint features extracted from it to distinguish the wearer from other speakers and improve attribution of records.

Voiceprint features and enrollment audio are stored locally on your phone for speaker recognition, sample playback, and voiceprint updates. In the App, go to “Settings → Voice ID” to enroll, view, play back, or delete enrolled samples. Deleting an individual sample also deletes its corresponding local voiceprint features, sample record, and enrollment audio. Enrollment samples are stored separately from ordinary audio caches and are not removed with ordinary transcription caches; you can delete them on that page when no longer needed. If a specific cloud feature needs to transmit speaker labels, recognition confidence, or other acoustic features, this will be explained before upload. The upload scopes for raw audio and acoustic features will be explained separately.

Voiceprints involve biometric information. We will explain their necessity and possible impacts and obtain separate or explicit consent when applicable law requires it. Turning off or deleting voiceprints does not prevent use of features that do not depend on them.

3.4 Photos, Visual Descriptions, and Visual Processing

When you enable device photography, actively upload an image, or select a photo, we process the relevant image, capture time, text you include, and recognized scene descriptions or visible text. Images may contain other people's faces, homes, screens, signs, documents, and other personal information.

Some images are first compressed, filtered, or processed for privacy locally. These measures can reduce exposure but cannot guarantee identification or masking of all personal information. When cloud image understanding is needed, after obtaining the appropriate authorization, we send necessary images or descriptions to expressly listed service providers.

You can refuse photo-related authorizations; image understanding, scene understanding, and corresponding automatic recording features may then be limited.

3.5 Chats, Records, Memories, and AI Outputs

We process text, attachments, conversation histories, notes, diaries, preferences, and related AI results that you enter, send, save, or import to respond to requests, organize records, retrieve history, and provide personalization you enable.

Depending on the task, Sein may select historical conversations, relevant transcripts, memory summaries, photo descriptions, or authorized contextual information as AI context. We limit context and do not attach your entire life history to every request by default merely to provide personalization. Cloud memory retrieval, insights, indexes, and generated results receive protection equivalent to the original private content; memory summaries, search results, or triggering reasons cannot bypass access restrictions.

When corresponding features are enabled, the phone can analyze speech rate, relative volume, fundamental frequency in Hz, pauses, speaking turns, candidate emotions, and audio events for authorized scene understanding and skill triggering. These features, triggering rules, reasons for matches, and results are also protected private content and are not basic information viewable in ordinary operations. Acoustic features do not necessarily constitute accurate judgments about emotion, identity, or health.

AI-inferred emotions, preferences, relationships, or life circumstances may be inaccurate. You can request correction or deletion of related personal information and turn off applicable personalization. We do not use these inferences to decide your eligibility for credit, employment, insurance, or other matters with similarly significant effects.

3.6 Location and Motion Status

After you enable location recording and grant appropriate permissions, Sein processes location coordinates, collection times, nearby place names or addresses, and motion status such as walking, riding in a vehicle, cycling, or remaining stationary to display routes, review places, and understand context. With background permission and the related features enabled, location processing may occur while the App is not in the foreground.

Whether location records remain local or are also uploaded to the cloud depends on the synchronization or cloud features and authorizations you separately enable. Granting system location permission does not automatically mean consenting to upload your entire location history. You can turn off corresponding processing through App settings and iOS permission management.

3.7 Health and Fitness Information

After you authorize connection to Apple Health, we read the categories you permit, which may include sleep, steps, workouts, active energy, exercise distance, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, cardio fitness, and mindfulness records, for health or fitness review features you enable.

We do not read all health data merely because the App supports health features. Availability of each data item depends on your authorization and device support. If health summaries need to be used for cloud AI processing, we will expressly explain the data scope, recipients, and health or fitness purposes and obtain appropriate authorization.

Health and fitness data is not used for advertising, marketing profiles, provision to data brokers, or data mining unrelated to health and fitness purposes. We will not expand its uses based solely on a general AI consent dialog.

3.8 Calendars, Reminders, and Other Content Sources

When you use a feature requiring calendars or reminders, after obtaining the appropriate permission, we read or write the titles, times, locations, and content you specify that are necessary to complete the task. For example, calendar events during a relevant period may help understand the current occasion; creating a reminder requires writing content you confirm.

Reading, writing, and sending to the cloud are each limited by the specific feature, system permissions, and applicable authorization. Refusing these permissions does not affect features that do not depend on calendars or reminders.

Entry points such as share menus, file pickers, and photo pickers process only content you choose to give Sein. If continuous reading or other broader features are provided in the future, we will explain them separately.

3.9 Sharing, External Tools, and Third-Party Services

When you actively share records, send links, use friend interactions, export files, or invoke external tools, your selected recipients receive information necessary to complete that action. Before sharing, please check whether content includes other people's or sensitive information.

Actions such as sending messages externally and creating calendar events follow that feature's confirmation process. Enabling a skill does not authorize it to read all information. If third-party skills or tools receive data as independent personal information controllers, we will provide appropriate disclosures and policy links before use.

3.10 Orders, Subscriptions, and Customer Support

If you purchase a device, membership, or other paid service, we process order numbers, products or entitlements, transaction status, payment channels, and contact information required for fulfillment. We process shipping or invoice information only when we actually undertake delivery or invoicing.

When payment is made through the App Store, Apple processes payment information under its own rules. Sein processes transaction receipts and status needed to confirm entitlements and does not directly collect complete payment card information.

When you contact customer support, we process the contact information, problem description, and necessary attachments you provide. Diagnostic records or content samples are collected only within the disclosed scope, with unrelated private content removed wherever possible.

3.11 Operational, Security, and Website Information

To maintain connections, troubleshoot, prevent abuse, and bill for services, we process necessary network addresses, request times, error codes, elapsed time, token usage, device and version information, security events, and push notification identifiers. General logs do not record private body content, model inputs or outputs, complete media links, acoustic feature values, original triggering rules, or memory summaries. Necessary operational status is limited to technical information such as error codes, connection status, versions, elapsed time, and task success or failure; it excludes emotion trends, location histories, and other private records.

The website may use necessary local storage to save language or other preferences. If we use nonessential analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies, we will disclose them and offer choices before use according to applicable rules. Nonessential analytics and cross-app tracking are not included in your consent to basic services.

Actual third-party SDKs, network services, and their processing scopes are listed in Appendix 1. This provision alone does not enable nonessential processing that has not been listed and appropriately authorized.

3.12 AI-Generated Content Labels

As required, we process service names or codes, content identifiers, and generation attributes needed for generated-content labels to indicate content sources, support compliance traceability, and prevent malicious tampering with labels. Necessary labels will not be used to publicly display your private identity, chats, or health information.

5. Cloud AI, Synchronization, and Service Providers

5.1 Protection Boundaries for Private Content and Enabling Cloud Features

We make protection by user keys, unreadability through the administration system, and authorized protected computing prerequisites for enabling cloud storage and processing of private content. The corresponding features will be enabled only after the relevant safeguards are available and verified. Local use or consent to this Policy does not automatically enable cloud processing.

The corresponding features must meet these boundaries: private content is stored encrypted with user keys; the Sein team cannot read your audio transcripts, photos, chats, diaries, memories, or other private records through the operational administration system; ordinary gateways, database administration, and operations environments do not hold keys capable of decrypting this content, and no viewing interface bypassing this boundary is provided.

Only verified and authorized protected computing environments may process necessary private content to complete designated tasks. Authorization is limited to the task purpose, permitted data, and recipients; enabling one feature does not expand it to your entire life history. The operational administration system displays only account information, orders, service usage, and necessary operational status, and does not display emotions, location histories, memory summaries, or other private content.

When content will be sent to a model provider, before transmission we will clearly disclose the recipient, purpose, data scope, processing region, and retention rules and obtain appropriate authorization. Model providers process content needed to complete the task as agreed. Unreadability through our administration system does not mean model providers do not process content you authorize us to send. You can refuse the corresponding processing; refusal affects only features dependent on it.

5.2 Cloud Storage, Synchronization, and Key Recovery

Cloud storage or synchronization occurs only after you enable the corresponding feature, with synchronization categories, historical migration scope, and retention rules explained beforehand. Private records, media, memories, and related retrieval indexes are stored encrypted. Turning off the feature stops subsequent synchronization; deletion of existing cloud data follows Section 10.

When these cloud features are enabled, the user's master key is managed by the user's devices. The cloud must not hold a plaintext master key that allows customer support to directly restore or read private records. When you authorize a cloud task, only a verified protected environment receives the decryption capability needed for that task.

Once the corresponding key recovery feature is provided, access to historical encrypted content on a new device requires confirmation by a trusted device or an officially provided encrypted recovery method. SMS sign-in, customer support verification, or an account reset alone does not give the team access to historical private content. If all authorized devices and recovery credentials are lost, historical encrypted content may be unrecoverable.

Our recovery approach uses confirmation by a trusted existing device or recovery credentials held by the user; specific steps are explained before the feature is enabled. Recovery credentials are not stored in the cloud as plaintext readable by the team. You can manage authorized devices and revoke future access. Revocation cannot remotely retrieve plaintext copies already exported or saved on that device.

Cloud tasks operate under time-limited authorization. After withdrawal, new authorizations and decryption stop, and running tasks stop subsequent processing through authorization checks and expiry mechanisms. Worker processes must obtain device authorization again after restarting. If authorization validity cannot be verified, decryption, content transmission, and publication of results do not continue. Data already sent to model providers or actively shared by you is handled under the deletion rules of the corresponding provider and sharing process.

5.3 Cloud Providers, Models, and Routing Services

Sein prioritizes Alibaba Cloud as its cloud service solution and may enable the following services according to disclosed features and service regions: Alibaba Cloud Bailian/Model Studio (including Qwen models), Amazon Web Services (AWS, including Amazon Bedrock), Microsoft Azure (including Azure OpenAI/Foundry), Vercel AI Gateway, and OpenRouter. Qwen is the name of a model family, not another independent contracting entity for cloud services.

These services play different roles: cloud infrastructure provides storage and computing, model hosting platforms perform inference, and gateways or aggregated routing services forward requests to the corresponding model recipients. When content is sent through a gateway, both the gateway and the actual downstream inference service are included in the processing list; disclosing only one layer is insufficient.

This Policy lists services that may be used. It does not mean all services are enabled or grant advance blanket authorization to send data to every named provider. Actual enabled services, recipient entities, purposes, fields, processing regions, and retention arrangements are specified in Appendix 1 and disclosures before feature activation. New routes receiving private content will not be enabled without appropriate disclosure and authorization.

Switching providers or routes must still comply with the data regions, purposes, and authorization scope disclosed to you. If these boundaries cannot be met, the affected cloud feature is suspended; failures or default fallbacks do not justify transferring private content to unauthorized recipients or regions.

5.4 Model Training, Fine-Tuning, and Provider Retention

Ordinary users' private content is not used for model training, fine-tuning, or human quality evaluation. Using AI generation features, synchronizing records, or accepting this Policy does not authorize participation in a data improvement program.

We require model providers acting as processors not to use private content received for normal services to train or fine-tune their models. Inference caches, security reviews, and stateful conversations you enable may have different retention rules, which will be specified individually in Appendix 1. No training does not mean no retention.

5.5 Voluntary Data Improvement Programs for Invited Users

We may invite some users to voluntarily participate in experience, research, or model improvement programs. Receiving an invitation does not mean you have agreed to participate. Only after you provide separate explicit consent and actively select and provide designated data will we receive and use it within that authorization.

Before participation, we will separately explain data categories, purposes, whether humans will view the data, whether it will be used for training or fine-tuning, recipients, retention periods, how to withdraw, and corresponding effects. Different purposes, including human review, training, and fine-tuning, must be clearly listed; unauthorized uses are prohibited. Refusing participation does not affect normal use of the services.

The program uses a separate data sharing and processing process and does not give the team access to your entire private database or master key. Once you actively share data, personnel within the authorized scope may read those copies. We manage them separately, restrict personnel and purposes, and delete them upon the agreed expiry.

You can withdraw from future participation. We will stop new program uses and handle source data and derived data already provided according to applicable requirements. If model training or fine-tuning is intended, before participation we must clearly explain how trained models will be handled, available deletion or unlearning measures, and their limitations. Deleting source files is not treated as equivalent to removing their influence from a model.

Each invitation-only program will provide a separate participation explanation and consent page specifying, before you submit data, the specific purposes, scope of personnel authorized to view it, retention periods, withdrawal methods, and rules for handling data and models where training or fine-tuning is involved. You can preview the data to be provided and decide whether to submit it. Without your separate explicit consent, program data is neither collected nor used. This Policy itself does not replace participation authorization.

7. Data Storage Regions and Cross-Border Processing

Your service region is determined by the service environment you register for or select and by express disclosure. Your entire record history will not automatically migrate merely because you travel, switch languages, or temporarily change IP addresses.

Before enabling each cloud feature, we show you the applicable data storage country or region, inference region, and support access arrangements involving personal information. The corresponding private-content upload or cross-border processing is not enabled until these matters are specified and appropriate authorization is obtained.

Services in mainland China and outside mainland China must have separately configured data environments. Storage, inference, and support access regions are explained separately and are not mixed merely because the same cloud provider is used. Where personal information is provided from mainland China to overseas recipients, we fulfill disclosure, separate consent, impact assessment, and applicable outbound transfer procedures as required by law. For restricted transfers involving the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or other regions, we use applicable adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses and necessary supplementary measures, or other lawful mechanisms.

Support access to basic information, authorized model processing, and program data separately shared by users are each assessed for access and cross-border requirements according to actual recipients. These arrangements do not grant the team decryption access to ordinary users' private content. Through our contact channels, you can learn about the cross-border safeguards applicable to your data and obtain explanations or copies with appropriate redaction of trade secrets and others' information.

9. How We Protect Information

For local and basic services, we use encrypted transmission, minimum necessary access, and security incident response measures proportionate to risk. Cloud features involving private content must also meet the key isolation and protected computing requirements in Section 5, covering ordinary gateways, queues, media, indexes, backups, and other paths. Corresponding features are not enabled before protection requirements are met.

Operations, customer support, and development personnel cannot decrypt ordinary users' private records merely for troubleshooting, product improvement, or because internal approval has been obtained. Troubleshooting prioritizes error codes, operational status, and synthetic test data. If content samples are truly necessary, you preview and actively choose to provide them. We process only copies you share and do not thereby obtain access to other records.

Invitation-only improvement programs and voluntarily submitted support materials have separate access and retention management. Authorized personnel may view them only for disclosed purposes. Account, order, and necessary operational information also receive least-privilege controls; being outside the private-record category does not make them public or freely usable.

You can protect information by locking devices, properly managing accounts and recovery credentials, checking sharing recipients, and updating software. These recommendations do not reduce our protection responsibilities.

If a personal information security incident occurs, we will take remedial measures, report as legally required, and, where necessary, explain the impact, measures taken, and available protective steps to affected users.

10. Your Choices and Rights

You can exercise rights through App settings, relevant feature pages, or the contact channels in this Policy. The absence of a self-service control does not affect your statutory rights; we will provide a practical channel handled by personnel.

10.1 Access, Correction, Copying, and Export

You can view or request personal information we hold, supplement or correct inaccurate information, and obtain readable, portable copies where applicable. You can view audio transcripts in “Settings → Data → Transcripts” and manage voiceprint samples in “Settings → Voice ID.” To copy or export other personal information, contact hi@seinpai.com. We will reasonably verify identity and assist with applicable exports. Private content that the team cannot decrypt should be exported through your authorized device; we will not ask you to surrender your master key or recovery credentials.

10.2 Deleting Information

You can delete corresponding records or request deletion of related information held by us and our processors. Deletion should cover raw audio, if any, audio transcripts, photos, chats, and memory summaries, retrieval indexes, insights, triggering records, and other related copies derived from them that no longer have an independent retention ground. Where data still has a lawful retention ground, we will explain the basis, scope, and period. We verify the scope, notify applicable recipients, and explain results. Shared copies lawfully downloaded or saved by other users may be beyond our ability to retrieve remotely, but we will assist within our control.

In “Settings → Data,” you can choose “Delete Chat History Only,” “Delete Insights Only,” or “Delete All Data” to delete local records within the scope confirmed on the page. Go to “Settings → Voice ID” to delete voiceprint samples.

Local deletion does not automatically mean cloud deletion. The product will clearly explain whether an action covers the phone, cloud, device, or a combination. You can request deletion of existing cloud copies at hi@seinpai.com. Processing the request does not require keys to decrypt private content; we can delete corresponding cloud copies through the association between accounts and encrypted objects. If applicable law requires a shorter period, that requirement prevails. Any scope that cannot immediately be deleted under law and its grounds will be explained separately.
